Official SEC tiebreaker order
  1. Head-to-head among the tied teams. In a tie of three or more where not everyone played everyone, a team advances only by beating all the others, and is dropped only by losing to all of them.
  2. Record against common conference opponents, meaning opponents every tied team played.
  3. Record against the highest-placed common opponent, then the next one down the standings until something separates.
  4. Combined conference winning percentage of each team's SEC opponents.
  5. Capped relative scoring margin across conference games, measured against each opponent's season scoring averages.
  6. Random draw.

When a step lifts one team clear, that team is in and the others restart at step A. When a step drops teams below a top group that is still too large, the dropped teams are out and the top group restarts. Two teams tied alone for first both go. The league office has the final say on interpretation.

Method: results and remaining schedules through Nov 7, 2026. All 1,048,576 outcomes of the last 20 SEC games are run through tiebreaker steps A to D, with tied common opponents in step C treated as one group. Ties that survive step D count as scoring-margin cases, not as in or out.

Model chance: each game's win probability comes from ESPN SP+ ratings dated Nov 8, 2026, with 2.5 points of home field and a 16.5-point standard deviation. That conversion is this page's assumption, not ESPN's.
